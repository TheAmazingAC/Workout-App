// Maintenance Targets

Global targets used in the Maintenance phase: next-week baselines, the velocity-card adherence panel, and the AI export. Protein is a dynamic equation — 0.8g/lb of current 7-day-average body weight (recomputed automatically, not a static number). Carbs are the caloric remainder — (kcal − protein×4 − fat×9) ÷ 4; hit RE-DERIVE CARBS after any change. Base plan: 2500 kcal · 60g fat · 15,000 step-eq. Calories default to a TDEE-derived suggestion — validated maintenance TDEE when available, else cut-phase TDEE as a provisional seed, else 2500. The field stays editable and the source shows below it; once you type your own value the suggestion never overwrites it.
